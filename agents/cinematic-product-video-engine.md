# Cinematic Product Video & Asset Synthesizer (`cinematic-product-video-engine`)

## Purpose
Governs the autonomous discovery, sourcing, generation, and embedding of **photorealistic real product videos and multi-angle visual assets** for all websites, applications, and landing pages. Eliminates reliance on raw, sterile 3D polygon meshes alone by elevating deliverables to Apple/Porsche-grade cinematic video standards.

---

## The 4-Phase Autonomous Pipeline

### Phase 1: Product Category & Cinematic Forensics
Whenever a product, vehicle, hardware item, or brand is specified:
1. **Category Deconstruction**: Classify product tier (e.g. Hyper-Muscle Automotive, Luxury Horology, Consumer Tech, Aerospace, Industrial Robotics).
2. **Hero Perspective Mapping**: Map the 5 mandatory product perspectives:
   - *Perspective 1*: Hero Front 3/4 Dynamic Angle.
   - *Perspective 2*: Low-slung side profile / aerodynamic silhouette.
   - *Perspective 3*: Mechanical heart (Engine bay, processor die, internal movements).
   - *Perspective 4*: Bespoke cockpit / interior / tactile interface.
   - *Perspective 5*: V-Max action track running / high-speed in-situ motion.

---

### Phase 2: Autonomous Web Video & Multi-Angle Harvesting
1. **Authoritative Media Querying**:
   - Query press media kits, manufacturer b-roll, open stock repositories (Pexels, Pixabay, Coverr, Wikimedia), and high-resolution multi-angle photography.
2. **Asset Validation Gate**:
   - Check resolution ($\ge 1080\text{p}$), framerate ($\ge 30\text{fps}$, ideally 60fps), and visual clarity.
   - Verify CORS access and direct MP4/WebM streamability for zero-lag browser playback.

---

### Phase 3: Autonomous 3D Video & Frame Synthesis (Fallback Protocol)
If authentic videos or specific camera angles are unavailable, restricted, or incomplete:
1. **Procedural 3D Video Rendering**:
   - Construct or load the high-fidelity 3D model with physical PBR materials (`MeshPhysicalMaterial`, metallic flakes, clearcoat lacquer, ambient occlusion).
   - Choreograph 60fps cinematic camera sweeps (orbital track, push-in zoom, low-angle tarmac rush).
2. **Scrollytelling Frame Sequencing (Apple Keynote Standard)**:
   - Export or render a synchronized sequence of frames into an HTML5 `<canvas>` video engine.
   - Allow the user to scrub, rotate, and interact with the video in real time based on scroll position or timeline drag.

---

### Phase 4: Seamless Luxury Integration & Dual-Engine Architecture
1. **Dual-Mode Presentation Engine**:
   - Every showcase must provide a single-click switch:
     - **Mode A: [🎥 Real Cinematic Video]** — Full-screen high-definition video of the product in action with asphalt rush, engine rumble, and broadcast polish.
     - **Mode B: [🏎️ 3D Mechanical Inspection]** — 360° interactive turntable with articulated mechanical opening parts (hood, doors, internal modules).
2. **Telemetry & Audio Sync**:
   - Video playback synchronizes with procedural Web Audio rumble, revving sounds, and dynamic HUD telemetry.

---

## Pre-Flight Verification Checklist
Before presenting any product showcase:
1. [ ] Is real cinematic video footage or high-framerate video animatic integrated?
2. [ ] Are multi-angle perspectives represented?
3. [ ] Does the video layer toggle seamlessly with the 3D interactive model?
4. [ ] Has the browser video playback been verified with exit code 0 and zero playback errors?
