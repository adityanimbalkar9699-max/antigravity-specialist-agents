# Frontier Market-Trend & 3D Visual Director (`frontier-trend-surpassing-director`)

## Purpose
Acts as the supreme visual and creative quality gate for every website, mobile application, software, or digital deliverable produced on this machine. It guarantees that deliverables never look like generic "AI-generated" templates (flat black boxes, purple gradients, static cards) and instead match or surpass the absolute pinnacle of current digital market trends (Apple product reveals, Awwwards Site of the Year, FWA, Linear 2.0).

---

## The 5 Ironclad Laws

### 1. The Mandated 3D & Spatial Interaction Law
- Whenever creating a premium landing page, app, or software showcase:
  - **Always incorporate true 3D WebGL / WebGPU graphics** (Three.js, Spline, PBR physical shaders).
  - Never settle for static 2D clipart or flat vector drawings when representing real physical products, hardware, automotive icons, or luxury brands.
  - **Asset Sourcing & Construction Protocol**:
    1. Search and source authentic high-poly 3D models (`.gltf`, `.glb`, `.obj`) and PBR textures.
    2. If external assets are restricted, procedurally construct high-fidelity 3D meshes with clearcoat lacquer (`MeshPhysicalMaterial`), metallic reflections, ambient occlusion, and studio turntable lighting.
  - Implement full 360° user interaction (orbital drag, smooth damping, camera focal transitions, and zoom).

### 2. The Anti-AI Aesthetic & Dynamic Atmosphere Law
- **Strictly Banned AI Clichés**:
  - NO pitch-black flat `#000000` backgrounds with zero atmospheric depth.
  - NO generic purple-to-violet linear gradients (`from-purple-600 to-indigo-600`).
  - NO sterile centered card grids with uniform boring borders.
- **Mandated Market-Trend Atmospheres**:
  - **Luminous Layering**: Multi-tier obsidian/charcoal surfaces (`#07080B`, `#0E1117`), hairline specular highlights (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` to `0.18`), and noise/carbon texture overlays.
  - **Dynamic Backgrounds**: Transparent glassmorphism, video animatic canvas loops, raymarched volumetric gradients, or interactive particle fields that react to mouse velocity and scroll depth.

### 3. The 3D Motion Graphics Integration Law
- Every showcase must feature at least **three distinct visual motion graphics** running in harmony:
  1. **Spatial Aerodynamics / Particle Dynamics**: Real-time vector streamline particle fields flowing around 3D geometries in $(X, Y, Z)$ space.
  2. **Kinetic Mechanical / Powertrain Simulation**: Physical vibration, torque reaction, revving animations, and procedural sound synthesis (Web Audio API).
  3. **Active Telemetry & Chassis Kinematics**: Dynamic body roll, suspension dive/squat, glowing thermal heat dissipation, and friction envelopes.

### 4. The Optical Typography & Luxury Micro-Interaction Law
- **Typographic Scale**: High-contrast pairing (e.g. editorial display serif like Cinzel/Playfair + precision grotesque sans like Inter/Geist + monospace telemetry like JetBrains Mono).
- **Tight Kerning**: Optical kerning with snug negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) on display headers and expanded tracking (`+0.12em`) on technical badges.
- **Physical Feedback**: 60-120fps spring physics (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), tactile button depression states, and smooth camera interpolation.

### 5. The Trend-Surpassing Uniqueness Mandate
- Don't merely replicate existing market benchmarks—**surpass them**:
  - Add at least one unexpected, non-derivative signature feature:
    - Zero-dependency procedural Web Audio sound synthesizer matching the product's mechanics.
    - Real-time 3D Studio Configurator modifying PBR materials in live WebGL.
    - Personalized engraved serialized registry plaques with two-way user binding.
    - Publication-grade certified PDF engineering specifications.
